# Agenda Capoeiragem

**Register:** brand

## Users

Two distinct personas using the product in very different contexts:

- **Educators and Organizers**: Capoeira mestres, instructors, and nucleo administrators. They run physical schools across Latin America, Europe, and beyond. Primary pain: managing attendance, finances, graduation records, and event logistics across multiple locations. Moderately technical. Access via mobile app daily; web admin occasionally.
- **Students and Travelers**: Active capoeiristas who train, compete, and travel. They want to find training spots globally, follow upcoming events (batizados, rodas, workshops), and maintain their capoeira identity (belt level, group affiliation). Mobile-first. Discover the platform via the landing page.

## Product Purpose

Agenda Capoeiragem is the management and community platform for the global capoeira ecosystem. The iOS/Android mobile app handles daily operations. The public web landing page is the discovery surface: its job is to convince educators to adopt the platform and give students a reason to join their school's community on it.

## Brand

- **Name:** Agenda Capoeiragem
- **Monogram:** a·c
- **Primary accent:** Emerald green (#10B981) — energy, movement, Brasil
- **Tone:** Bold and direct. Community-over-product. Not corporate, not startup-bro, not sports-app generic.
- **Cultural context:** Rooted in Afro-Brazilian martial art tradition. Global community. Serious tool, human warmth.
- **Anti-references:** Generic SaaS cream (Notion, Linear aesthetic), dark developer tools, Latin stereotype carnival colors, aggressive fitness-app style

## Strategic principles

- Real data (actual nucleos, educators, countries) anchors credibility in the hero
- Beta phase: registration form is the primary conversion action
- Bilingual ES/PT with EN — Latin American and Brazilian audience primary
- Platform value proposition splits cleanly: tools for educators, discovery for students
